What I do

I design practical, testable geospatial data workflows that turn messy real-world inputs into documented, analysis-ready datasets. I prioritize clear schemas, quality assurance, reproducibility, and privacy-preserving aggregation.

  • Ingest and standardize tabular and spatial data, including ACS, TIGER/Line, and local administrative datasets
  • Transform datasets through normalization, spatial joins, feature engineering, and tract-level aggregation
  • Validate outputs using schema checks, missingness audits, and geometry QA
  • Communicate results through maps, research writing, and lightweight interactive tools
